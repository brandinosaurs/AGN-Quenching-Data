# AGN-Quenching-Data
The graph depicts the analysis of log stellar Mass to log star formation rate/yr. AGN's are depicted in green, and the two lines with similar slopes indicate the total line of best fit for all stars and the line of best fit for quasars. The line with the largest slope, as demonstrated by the equation (x-11), is the threshold of AGN quenching. Quasars below the line exhibit quenching while quasars above the line do not.
